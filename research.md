## Research

My research interests broadly lie in the fields of <span style="color:#DC267F">music cognition</span> and <span style="color:#DC267F">psycholinguistics</span>. My current research concerns the perception and production of speech and song, where I'm the project manager and funded graduate researcher on an [NSF Grant](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024nsf....2419763P/abstract) investigating top-down and bottom-up factors that contribute to such abilities.

Below are some of the posters I've presented on this work.

### Posters

- **Proy, M.**, Ali, R., Pruitt, T. A., & Pfordresher, P. Q. (2026, November). Context effects in the perception and vocal imitation of speech and song. Poster accepted for the 22nd Annual NeuroMusic Conference. Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. [[poster](/assets/Proy_NeuroMusic_Poster_v2.pdf)]
- **Proy, M.**, & Pfordresher, P. Q. (2026, July). Phonetic constraints on pitch encoding along a speech-to-song continuum. Poster presented at the Society for Music Perception and Cognition Conference. Evanston, Illinois. [[poster](/assets/MP_SMPC_Poster_v3.pdf)]
- **Proy, M.**, & Pfordresher, P. Q. (2026, May). Manipulations of pitch stability transform speech into song. Poster presented at the 2026 APS Annual Convention. Barcelona, Spain. [[poster](/assets/MP_APS_BBSS_Poster_v3.pdf)]
