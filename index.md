## About

Hello!

I'm a Master's student in the Department of Psychology at the University at Buffalo, and a graduate research assistant and lab manager of the [Auditory Perception and Action Lab](https://ubwp.buffalo.edu/apal-pfordresher/) supervised by [Peter Pfordresher](https://www.uakron.edu/search/profiles/detail?profileId=5K8BNkJA8B5k32MAZa18TR0jA3YVJmC387Q8AR7E2mK8WCK9HQAJ4@JQJWQp02MUQ55CD6IfB6LHDm1JHQLjBZ7R0iN2XOY.S2H6O9GO7GM80MB4Bp) and [Tim Pruitt](https://arts-sciences.buffalo.edu/psychology/faculty/faculty-directory/pruitt.html). Previously, I completed a BA in Philosophy and Psychology at the University at Buffalo in 2025.

My research interests broadly lie in the fields of <span style="color:#785EF0">music cognition</span> and <span style="color:#DC267F">psycholinguistics</span>. My current research concerns the perception and vocal pitch imitation of speech and song, where I'm the project manager and funded graduate researcher on an [NSF Grant](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024nsf....2419763P/abstract) investigating top-down and bottom-up factors that contribute to such abilities.
