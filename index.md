## About

Hello! I am a Master's student in the Department of Psychology at the University at Buffalo.

I'm a graduate research assistant in the [Auditory Perception and Action Lab](https://ubwp.buffalo.edu/apal-pfordresher/) supervised by Peter Pfordresher and Tim Pruitt.

I completed a BA in Philosophy and Psychology at the University at Buffalo in 2025.

My research interests broadly lie in the fields of music cognition and psycholinguistics. My current research concerns the perception and vocal pitch imitation of speech and song, where I'm the project manager of an [NSF Grant](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024nsf....2419763P/abstract) investigating top-down and bottom-up factors that contribute to such abilities.
